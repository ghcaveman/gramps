NARA Catalog search notes
=========================

What the National Archives Catalog API v2 search (GET /records/search,
parameter q) accepts, found by running nara_query_syntax_probe.py on
Nov 28, 2026. Every count below is for "John Smith" with
dateRange=1879 TO 1973 and limit=3.


What works
----------

One exact phrase in double quotes.
    "John Smith"                      45,725 hits
    John Smith (loose words)       1,078,648 hits
    Loose words match any record that has both words anywhere, including
    the machine-read (OCR) text of its images, so a phrase is far tighter.

Surname first finds the most.
    "Smith John"                     108,220 hits
    Catalog titles mostly name people surname first ("Smith, John W.",
    "WHITE Howard", "White, Howard Seymour - Canada"). The comma is
    ignored, so "Smith John" matches "Smith, John" and "Smith, John W.".

A space between terms means AND: every term must appear.
    "John Smith" pension              17,832 hits
    Use this to narrow by place or keyword: "White Howard" Montgomery.

A NAID on its own finds that record.


What does not work
------------------

OR is read as an ordinary word, not an operator.
    "John Smith" OR "Smith John"           4 hits
    (records that have both phrases and the word "or")
    The same with or without dateRange.

The pipe | acts as AND, not OR.
    "John Smith" | "Smith John"          680 hits
    (fewer than either phrase alone)

Proximity ~N does not give an either-order search.
    "John Smith"~2                    43,104 hits
    (slightly fewer than "John Smith", not more)

Brackets do not group.
    ("John Smith" | "Smith John") pension     0 hits
    ("John Smith" OR "Smith John") AND pension
        HTTP 200 but text/html: the catalog's web page, not JSON. The
        client then fails with "NARA catalog returned invalid JSON"; it
        now logs the status, content type and start of the body instead.

So one request can ask for only one form of a name. Finding both "Smith
John" and "John Smith" takes two requests.

The dateRange parameter does not seem to filter.
    A search with dateRange=1847 TO 1925 returned 1930 and 1940 census
    schedules and a record titled "..., Year: 1942"; one with 1879 TO 1973
    returned Civil War draft registers (1863-1865). The search window now
    leaves out records whose years (from the title, or else the record's
    date) lie wholly outside the checked years, and says how many.


How GWizard uses this
---------------------

Multi-Archive Record Scan (gramps/plugins/tool/gwizardnaraquerybuilder.py,
build_primary_nara_query): one request per person, the birth surname and
first given name as one phrase, surname first: "White Howard". Middle
names are left out, so records without them still match. The person's
birth-to-death years go in dateRange.

NARA search window (gramps/gui/gwizard/gwizardnaralauncher.py,
build_nara_query): one request per Search click.
    - Name: one phrase, surname first; with Middle Name checked the
      middle name follows the given name: "White Howard Kingston".
    - Married Name checked: the married surname goes in the phrase and
      the birth surname is a further word: "Smith Mary" Kreizer.
    - Exact name: the name in the order written: "Howard Kingston White".
    - Cities and keywords: further words (AND).
    - Keywords that are only a NAID: that record alone.
    - Nicknames in quotes or brackets in the given name ("King") are left
      out of the First and Middle Name boxes.
    - Results dated wholly outside the checked years are left out (a
      record asked for by its NAID is always shown).

Reading names from titles (gramps/gen/gwizard/gwizardnaramatch.py) handles
"Smith, John W.", "Approved Pension Application File for Mary Ann Smith",
"SMITH John" index cards and "Smith John William -- [Service Number] ..."
Navy service files. Titles such as "Ohio, 8th Congressional District, Class
No. 1" (Civil War draft registers) and "..., Application for Enrollment
with the California Indians" are not read as names.

Known gap: a record titled given name first ("Mary Kreizer Smith, Widow
of ...") is missed by a surname-first search. In the window, tick Exact
name and search again to try the other order.


Not yet checked
---------------

- Whether some other date parameter does filter (dateRange appears not
  to; see above). A probe comparing hit counts with and without it, and
  with other parameter names, would tell.
- Whether a field-specific search (title only, creator only) is possible.
- Whether the * wildcard works (the window's Keywords tooltip says NARA
  accepts AND, OR, NOT, * and phrases; AND by space works, OR does not).
- Upper/lower case and accented letters in phrases.

Each probe costs one of the key's monthly queries, and the GWizard usage
counter does not see them.
